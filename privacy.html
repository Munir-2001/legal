Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This app is a personal scheduling tool run by Munir Abbasi. It is used only to publish content to social media accounts that I own. It is not offered to other users.

What it stores

Access tokens that the platforms issue when I connect my own accounts, the posts and media I schedule, their scheduled times, and basic statistics the platforms report about my posts (for example views, likes, comments, shares and follower counts).

Where it is stored

On my own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deletion

To request deletion of any data, email munir@munirabbasi.me. Disconnecting an account in the app removes its tokens.
